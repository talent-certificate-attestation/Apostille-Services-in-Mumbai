## What is an apostille?

An apostille is a certificate issued under the Hague Apostille Convention of 5 October 1961. India has been a member of the Convention since 2005. It is used for public documents intended for countries where the Convention applies.

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) provides apostille for eligible educational and personal documents, including degrees, diplomas, birth certificates, marriage certificates, affidavits and powers of attorney. An apostille is not a verification of the document’s contents; it authenticates the origin of the public document.

Check the destination country’s status and requirements before selecting apostille. Some destinations or document types may require a different legalization process.

## Which documents may need apostille?

The documents required depend on the purpose of travel and the receiving organisation. Common categories include:

- **Educational documents:** degree certificates, diplomas, marksheets and academic records.
- **Personal documents:** birth certificates, marriage certificates, affidavits and powers of attorney.
- **Other public documents:** certain official records required for immigration, employment, education or legal matters.

Ask the receiving institution or authority which documents it requires and whether originals or specific supporting records must be submitted.

## Maharashtra document verification before MEA apostille

Documents generally need preliminary authentication by the designated authority in the state or union territory where they were issued before MEA apostille.

The required authority depends on the document category. Educational documents may need authentication through the relevant education authority; personal documents may require verification by the designated state department; and commercial documents may require authentication through a Chamber of Commerce.

For Maharashtra-issued documents, confirm the current authentication route with the relevant state authority before submission. A university certificate and a personal certificate should not automatically be treated as having the same preliminary requirements.

## The apostille process in Mumbai

For documents not covered by the e-Sanad online process, the MEA describes a two-stage route:

1. **Pre-authentication:** The document is authenticated by the designated state or other competent authority.
2. **MEA apostille:** The document is submitted through an authorised service provider for apostille processing.

MEA apostille and attestation services have been decentralised to designated Branch Secretariats and Regional Passport Offices, including Mumbai. Applicants should verify the current submission channel and requirements before applying.

Some documents may be eligible for e-Sanad, the MEA’s online verification and attestation service. Eligibility depends on whether the document and its preliminary authentication can be verified through the system.

## Common apostille preparation mistakes

A few checks can help avoid unnecessary delays:

- **Choosing apostille without checking the destination:** Confirm that the receiving country accepts apostille for the intended document.
- **Skipping preliminary authentication:** Verify the designated authority for the document’s issuing state and category.
- **Assuming every document follows one route:** Educational, personal and commercial documents may have different requirements.
- **Submitting incomplete details:** Check names and document information against supporting identification.
- **Ignoring original-document requirements:** Confirm whether the original certificate must be submitted.
- **Relying on outdated instructions:** Recheck current requirements through official authorities before submission.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is apostille the same as embassy attestation?

No. Apostille is used for eligible public documents intended for countries where the Hague Apostille Convention applies. Other destinations may require normal attestation or legalization.

### Can documents issued outside Maharashtra be apostilled in Mumbai?

The route depends on the document’s place of issuance and the designated authority responsible for preliminary authentication. Confirm the appropriate jurisdiction before submitting the document in Mumbai.

### Does an apostille verify the contents of a certificate?

No. Apostille authenticates the origin of the public document, such as the signature, signatory’s capacity and seal or stamp. It does not certify the truth of the document’s contents.

### Can I apply for apostille online?

Some documents may be eligible through the MEA’s e-Sanad portal. Eligibility depends on whether the document and its pre-authentication can be verified through the system.

### How long does apostille take?

Processing time depends on the document, preliminary authentication and submission channel. Confirm the current timeline after the document route is identified.
